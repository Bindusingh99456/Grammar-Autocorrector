Hybrid Architecture & Data Isolation

The system combines deterministic rule-based correction with a sequence-to-sequence model:

  • Linguistic Rules: Deterministic checks for subject-verb agreement, auxiliary verbs (is/are, has/have, does/do), and indefinite articles (a/an).
  • Cloud GPU Model Training: Staged LoRA fine-tuning of CoEdIT-Large performed via Google Colab / Kaggle; local server executes rules and inference.
  • Zero Data Leakage: Automated overlap auditing confirms 0 overlapping sentences between training data and test benchmarks (JFLEG test and W&I+LOCNESS dev).

Known Failures & Linguistic Boundaries

In academic reporting, documenting genuine linguistic edge cases is essential. The following failure modes are documented:

1. Long-Distance Relative Clauses

"The teacher who prepares five different lessons walk to school." — Intervening clauses separate subject and verb by multiple tokens, bypassing local adjacency windows.

2. Noun-Verb POS Homonym Ambiguity

"Her daily records show remarkable progress." — Isolated perceptual taggers can misclassify noun homonyms as verbs (or vice-versa).

3. Subjunctive Mood Constructions

"I demand that he go immediately." — Mandative subjunctive demands base-form verbs with third-person subjects, which simple agreement rules would incorrectly flag.

4. Contextual Homophone Substitutions

"Their going to the market." — Homophones like "their" vs "they're" are syntactically valid words that require full semantic attention rather than morphological inflection.
